cities.tsv and meta.json are adapted from the GeoNames geographical database by GeoNames
(https://www.geonames.org), files cities15000.zip, admin1CodesASCII.txt and countryInfo.txt from
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/ (downloaded 28 September 2026).

Changes made: columns reduced; names folded (lower case, no accents or punctuation) for search;
coordinates rounded to 2 decimals; historical, abandoned and destroyed places removed; alternate names kept
only for cities of 1 million people or more; and a place added for each IANA time zone that has no city in
the data, from zone.tab (public domain). Generated by tools/make_cities.py.
